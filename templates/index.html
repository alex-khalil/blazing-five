Blazing 5 Pick'em Rules

1. Buy-in: $25 entry fee per player creates the season prize pool.

2. 5 Weekly ATS Picks: Each participant selects 5 games against the spread each week.

3. The LOCK (±2 wins/losses): Exactly 1 of your 5 weekly picks MUST be designated as your LOCK. Regular picks are worth ±1, while the LOCK is worth ±2.

4. 🔥 Blazing Week: Each player must declare a Blazing Week once per season. During this week, ALL 5 picks become LOCKs worth ±2 each (up to 10 points). If undeclared, it defaults to Week 18.

5. Must-Pick Game: Each week has 1 mandatory featured game that EVERY participant must include in their 5 selections. An assigned player chooses this game.

6. Perfect Week Bounty: Going 6-0 (or 5-0) earns a +$5 cash bounty from the pot. A 0-6 winless week pays a $5 penalty into the pot.

7. Season Over/Under: 5 team win total picks (1 Lock) selected before Week 1 kickoff.

8. Payouts: 1st: 50%, 2nd: 25%, 3rd: 12.5%, 4th: 7.5%, 5th: 5%.
